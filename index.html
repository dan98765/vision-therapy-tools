Vision Therapy Tools

Small exercises that run in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and your settings stay on this device.

Exercises

  • Hart Chart Generator

    Printable rows of random letters for saccade and near-far focusing practice.

  • Matched Hart Pair

    A small chart and a large chart with the same letters, for near-far practice.

  • Macdonald Form Field Cards

    Printable concentric rings of letters for peripheral awareness and form recognition practice.

Utilities

  • Letter Size Calculator

    Work out how tall a letter must be for a given viewing distance and visual angle, and send it to the charts.

  • Screen Calibration

    Match a credit card or a sheet of paper to your screen to find how many pixels make a real millimetre.

Not medical advice. Use exercises as directed by your vision therapist.
