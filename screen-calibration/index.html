Screen Calibration

Match something of known size to the screen to find how many pixels make a real millimetre

Step 1: choose what to measure against

A credit, bank or ID card is the same size everywhere. A longer reference, such as a sheet of paper, gives a more precise result if it fits on your screen.

Step 2: make the box match

Hold your object flat against the screen with one edge lined up with the left edge of the box. Move the slider until its other edge lines up with the right edge of the box.
